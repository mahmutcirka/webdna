# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The summary below does not replace the full text.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in WebDNA a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- Focus on evidence, especially when discussing detection rules and other people's websites.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give credit.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using WebDNA reports to accuse or shame individuals or site owners.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the project maintainers through the contact listed in the repository's GitHub profile or security policy. Maintainers will review reports promptly and confidentially and may take any action they deem appropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
